### **Chapter 10. Search other Quake archives and communities**

Introduce other people and organisations that have already collected Quake files.

Examples include:

- Quaddicted.
- Shub-Hub.
- Gamers.org archives.
- Other community-maintained Quake archives.
- Personal FTP servers.
- Public and private cloud collections.
- Individual collectors and archivists.

Teach the learner how to identify relevant collections and investigate whether they contain missing releases.

An important part of this chapter is community engagement.

Other Quake archivists might have files that are not publicly indexed. They might be willing to search their collections, provide copies or share information about other collectors.

Building relationships with other archivists can be one of the most productive parts of the process.

**Outcome:** The learner understands that archival research is also a community activity and knows how to find and approach other collectors.
