# Final Word

Thank you for reading **The Quake Archivist's Handbook**.

I hope you've found something interesting, learned a few new techniques, or discovered a resource that helps you in your own archival adventures.

Archiving old Quake modifications is part research, part detective work, and part treasure hunt. You never quite know what you'll uncover, where the next lead might take you, or what forgotten piece of Quake history might still be out there waiting to be found.

I hope this book has encouraged you to join the hunt.

## Keep in Touch

I'd love to hear from you.

If you've started an archive of your own, please get in touch. I'd love to see what you're working on, what you've discovered, and how you're approaching the challenge.

If you're stuck trying to track down a particular file, release, or piece of information, let me know. I may be able to suggest somewhere else to look or help you think through the problem.

If there's a Quake modification you'd like me to investigate, I'd be happy to hear about it. I'm always interested in a new mystery, especially one involving a forgotten or hard-to-find modification.

And if you've developed better techniques, discovered useful resources, or have suggestions for improving the methods described in this book, I'd love to learn from you. This is an ongoing process, and I'm certain there are many approaches and resources I've yet to discover.

## The Hunt Continues

There are still countless Quake modifications, maps, tools, websites, and other pieces of history waiting to be rediscovered and preserved.

Some are hiding on forgotten FTP servers. Others are buried in old CD-ROM collections, archived websites, or personal backups that haven't been opened in decades. Some may survive only in the memories and hard drives of the people who created them.

Every file recovered, every release documented, and every forgotten project brought back into the light is a small victory.

And perhaps the most exciting part is knowing that something remarkable might still be out there, just waiting for someone curious enough to go looking.

Thanks again for reading.

**Happy hunting!**
