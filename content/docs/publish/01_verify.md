### **Chapter 13. Verify, classify and connect discoveries**

This chapter brings the research together.

Teach the learner how to:

- Identify duplicate files.
- Distinguish different releases.
- Compare files with different names.
- Compare files with identical names.
- Verify archive contents.
- Identify incomplete or corrupted downloads.
- Connect files to known releases.
- Identify files with uncertain origins.
- Assess conflicting release dates.
- Record confidence and uncertainty.
- Preserve original files independently of derived or repaired copies.

This is also where the learner should revisit their chronology and release catalogue.

For example, a newly discovered file might turn out to be an earlier version than any previously known release.

It might also reveal that a release previously thought to be a complete package was actually an incremental patch.

Research is iterative. Every new discovery can change the interpretation of earlier evidence.

**Outcome:** The learner can turn recovered material into a coherent and defensible archival record.
